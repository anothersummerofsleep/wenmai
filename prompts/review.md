# Review pass (optional, LLM-assisted)

You are reviewing a finished English chapter against the novel's context and style. Report problems;
do not rewrite the chapter here.

## Check for
- Terminology / name drift: any rendering that disagrees with the canonical context records.
- Continuity errors against the recent timeline and relationships.
- Over-annotation (notes that no trigger justifies) or under-annotation (a clear idiom/pun/allusion
  left unexplained on its first appearance).
- Style-guide violations: wrong honorific forms, merged paragraphs, tense slips, re-coined terms.
- Machine-translation awkwardness: literal calques, stiff word order, dropped subjects.

## Output
A short bulleted list. For each item: the location (quote a few words), what is wrong, and the fix.
If the chapter is clean, say so in one line. Keep it terse and actionable.
