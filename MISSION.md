# Wenmai / 文脉

## Tagline

**Translate the words. Preserve the thread.**

## Mission

Wenmai exists to make long-form translation more faithful to the story as a whole.

A good translation should remember who a character was hundreds of chapters ago, why a particular
title matters, how a technique or concept was previously translated, what relationships have
changed, and when apparently simple wording contains a joke, cultural reference, double meaning, or
linguistic nuance that cannot be carried directly into another language.

Wenmai combines persistent narrative context, terminology memory, linguistic analysis, retrieval,
consistency checking, and human-reviewable translation workflows so that meaning is not lost simply
because it cannot be mapped directly from one language to another.

The project begins with Chinese-to-English webnovel translation, where idioms, homophones, classical
references, naming conventions, cultural context, and recurring terminology make context
particularly important.

But the problem Wenmai is designed to solve is broader.

Different languages carry meaning differently. Future language support should be able to bring its
own linguistic knowledge while sharing the same underlying systems for narrative memory,
terminology, context retrieval, translation history, consistency, annotations, and evaluation.

Wenmai is therefore not built around the assumption that every story is Chinese.

It is built around the assumption that every long story has a memory.

The objective is not merely to translate sentences.

**The objective is to carry the 文脉 forward.**
