# Chapter 1: The Old Fox

Senior Brother Zhang entered the hall.

Li Yun lowered his voice to the junior sister beside him. "Don't be fooled by that mild face. This
one is an 老狐狸, an 'old fox' [a set expression for someone extremely shrewd and cunning, not a
literal comment on his age]. Be careful dealing with him."

Up on the dais the elder stroked his beard and smiled, then spoke slowly. "You certainly have a lot
of qi."
[Wordplay: 气 can mean "qi/energy" but also "temper" or "cockiness." The elder is deliberately
playing on both. He may be praising the depth of Li Yun's cultivation, or needling him for being
so full of himself at such a young age. Li Yun cannot tell which.]

Li Yun froze, unsure how to answer.

He cupped his hands. "This junior would not dare."

The elder nodded, then turned his gaze to Azure Cloud Peak beyond the window and said nothing for a
long while.
